\begin{theorem}\label{th:vanishing-application}
Let $X$ satisfy Conditions A and B. Then the obstruction group $O(X)$ vanishes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Construction~C identifies $O(X)$ with the degree-two term of the complex $K(X)$. Conditions A and B verify the hypotheses of the External Vanishing Theorem [Reference E, Theorem 4]. Applying that theorem gives the conclusion. We do not reproduce the proof of the External Vanishing Theorem here.
\end{proof}
