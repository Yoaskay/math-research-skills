\section{Effective descent}

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category fibered in groupoids on a site $(\mathcal{S},\tau)$.
For a covering family $\{U_i\to X\}$, a descent datum consists of objects
$E_i\in\mathcal{C}(U_i)$ and isomorphisms between their pullbacks to
$U_i\times_X U_j$ satisfying the cocycle condition on triple fiber products.
It is effective if it is induced by an object of $\mathcal{C}(X)$.

\begin{definition}
The fibered category $\mathcal{C}$ is a stack for $\tau$ if descent data for every
$\tau$-covering family are effective and morphisms satisfy descent.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Effective descent]\label{th:effective-descent}
If $\mathcal{C}$ is a stack for $\tau$, then for every $\tau$-cover
$\{U_i\to X\}$ the restriction functor
\[
  \mathcal{C}(X)\longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Desc}_{\mathcal{C}}(\{U_i\to X\})
\]
is an equivalence of categories.
\end{theorem}

The theorem is used by constructing objects on a cover, checking the pairwise
compatibilities and cocycle condition, and then descending them to the base.
The proof is immediate from the stack axiom and is not expanded here.
